# Small business purchasing scenario

WHEN the buyer reviews replenishment, THE SYSTEM SHALL show a one-warehouse,
30-product, four-supplier synthetic scenario over a fixed 30-day window.

WHEN starting inventory is evaluated, THE SYSTEM SHALL deduct reservations and
use remaining demand that excludes the already allocated customer demand.

WHEN receipts are evaluated, THE SYSTEM SHALL count only confirmed open receipts
dated after the snapshot and on or before the planning end; overdue, cancelled,
and later receipts shall remain visible with explicit exclusion reasons.

WHEN suppliers are selected, THE SYSTEM SHALL respect approved sourcing,
known lead time, arrival within the window, confirmed deliverable capacity,
minimum order quantity, and whole purchase packs. Units are pieces; pack sizes
convert ordering units to pieces. Landed prices are synthetic USD cents per piece.

WHEN complete coverage is impossible, THE SYSTEM SHALL report the unavoidable
requirement shortfall and distinguish forecast-demand shortfall from safety-stock
shortfall. No fictitious perfect-coverage result shall be published.

WHEN early demand could precede replenishment, THE SYSTEM SHALL flag a dated
buyer exception. End-of-window optimization is not a daily service guarantee.

WHEN recommendations are published, THE SYSTEM SHALL validate foreign keys,
dates, inventory, pack multiples, MOQ, capacity, costs, and independently verify
optimal per-product cost; the agent shall read the deployed new ontology.

Original source data and legacy network-demo artifacts shall be retained. The
new operational facts are explicitly synthetic, not corrections to real records.
The scenario and results are fixed snapshots, regenerated by an explicit command.
Agent updates shall preserve agent identity. No real orders are executed.
